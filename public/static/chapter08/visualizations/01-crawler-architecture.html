Why It Loops

Each fetched page yields new hyperlinks. After deduplication (already-seen URLs are dropped), the survivors are pushed back into the frontier — so the crawler never runs out of work until the frontier empties. DNS resolution during Fetch is a classic bottleneck, so crawlers cache DNS results aggressively.
