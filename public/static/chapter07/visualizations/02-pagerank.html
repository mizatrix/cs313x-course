The PageRank Update
PR(p) = (1 − d)/N + d · Σq→p PR(q) / L(q)

N = number of pages (5). L(q) = number of out-links from page q. The (1−d)/N term is the random-jump (teleport) probability; the second term is rank flowing through links.
