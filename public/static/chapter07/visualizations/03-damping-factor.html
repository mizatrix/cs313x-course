Damping Factor & Teleport

The damping factor d sets how often the random surfer follows a link vs. teleports to a random page. Drag the slider and watch a rank sink trap—or release—PageRank mass.

💡
Try This: Toggle the dangling-node sink ON, then push d → 1.0. Watch almost all rank pile onto node D. Now set d = 0 — every page becomes exactly uniform. The sweet spot is around d ≈ 0.85.
