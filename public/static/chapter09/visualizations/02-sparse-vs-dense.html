Key Insight

Sparse retrieval needs the exact word to appear — fast, interpretable, but blind to synonyms (the vocabulary mismatch problem). Dense retrieval maps text into a vector space where meaning is geometry, so "car" sits near "automobile" even with no shared letters. Hybrid systems combine both.
